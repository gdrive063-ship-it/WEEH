How WEEH works

WiFi routers and wireless devices surround us with tiny radio waves. WEEH captures a small fraction of those ambient RF signals with a tuned antenna and converts the high-frequency alternating signal into steady direct current. A micropower booster raises that voltage so it can gradually charge a storage capacitor, ready to power low-energy sensors without a battery.
